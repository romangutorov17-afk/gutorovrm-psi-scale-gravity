## 1. Introduction

The standard ΛCDM model explains many observations but requires dark matter. MOND-like approaches reproduce many galaxy rotation curves, but they often face difficulties in local gravitational tests and in cosmology.

ψ-scale gravity is designed to:

- explain galaxy rotation curves without dark matter;
- remain consistent with local tests;
- introduce natural screening through a scale-dependent α(r);
- connect gravity with holographic scaling.
